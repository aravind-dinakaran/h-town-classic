About the H-Town Classic

The H-Town Classic is a 3x3 youth basketball tournament celebrating competition and community in the Greater Houston area. Brought to you by Academy Sports + Outdoors in partnership with Nike and Jordan Brand, the event brings together up to 128 teams across 4 divisions in a double-elimination format. All proceeds benefit the Boys & Girls Clubs of Greater Houston, making this tournament both a celebration of the game and a community investment.

The H-Town Classic will be emceed by Kiotti Brown, voice of the Houston Rockets, and Tausha Sanders, founder of Her Grails. Together, they will provide live commentary and keep the energy high throughout the tournament weekend.

The H-Town Classic is a 32-team double-elimination tournament with 4 divisions for middle and high school boys and girls. Each team may have up to 4 players and must have a minimum of 3 players at registration.

The H-Town Classic features 4 divisions:

Tournament Rules

Key Rule: The target score for all games is 20 points, meaning the first team to reach 20 points within 20 minutes of play is declared the winner.

The H-Town Classic follows 3x3 basketball rules with the following specifications:

  • Game Length: First to 20 points OR 20 minutes, whichever comes first.
  • Game Format: 3x3 (3 players per team on court)
  • Bracket: Double-elimination format

For complete rules and regulations, consult the official H-Town Classic rulebook here.

3x3 basketball is a faster paced, half-court game with 3 players per side instead of 5. Key differences include:

  • Played on half a court
  • Smaller rosters (3-4 players per team)
  • Faster pace and quicker game resolution
  • Players return to the back line after scores
  • 2-point line at the 3-point distance

In double-elimination, teams get a second chance after their first loss. After losing once, teams move to the "losers' bracket" and continue playing. Teams are eliminated after losing twice. This format continues until a champion emerges from the winners' bracket and a runner-up from the losers' bracket.

Players must be within the grade range for their division:

  • Middle School Division: Grades 6-8
  • High School Division: Grades 9-12

Forfeit policies are detailed in the official H-Town Classic rules and tournament guidelines. Teams should plan to have their roster available for all scheduled games to avoid forfeits.
